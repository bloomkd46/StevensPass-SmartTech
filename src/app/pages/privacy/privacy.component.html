We collect limited technical data to operate and improve this service:

2. How We Use Your Data

Data collected is used solely to:

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties beyond the services listed above.

3. Cookies

Firebase / Google Analytics may set cookies to distinguish unique visitors. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect site functionality.
